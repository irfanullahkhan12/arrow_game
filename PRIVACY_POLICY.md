# Privacy Policy - Arrows Neon

**Last Updated:** September 14, 2026

## Introduction

Arrows Neon ("the Game") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our mobile game.

## Information We Collect

### 1. Data Stored Locally on Your Device
The Game stores the following information locally on your device:
- **Game Progress**: Current level, completed levels, and game statistics
- **Player Profile**: Coins, power-ups, daily streak, and game settings
- **Game State**: Current board configuration and widget game count
- **Preferences**: Sound settings, theme preferences, and other user choices

This data is stored using your device's local storage (shared preferences) and remains on your device. We do not have access to this locally stored data.

### 2. Analytics and Advertising Data
- **Advertising ID**: The Game may access your device's advertising identifier for ad personalization and measurement purposes
- **Ad Interaction Data**: Information about your interactions with advertisements (impressions, clicks, conversions)
- **Performance Data**: Crash reports and anonymous usage statistics to improve game performance

### 3. Optional AI API Usage
If you choose to enable the AI-powered board generation feature by providing an API key:
- **API Key**: Your API key is used only for generating board outlines and is not stored or transmitted to our servers
- **Generated Content**: Board shapes and designs are generated locally after receiving parameters from the AI service

## How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information for:
- **Game Functionality**: To save your progress, settings, and provide a personalized gaming experience
- **Advertising**: To display relevant advertisements and measure ad performance
- **Game Improvement**: To analyze usage patterns, fix bugs, and improve game features
- **Widget Functionality**: To enable home-screen widget gameplay and daily reminders

## Third-Party Services

The Game uses the following third-party services:

### 1. Google Mobile Ads (AdMob)
- **Purpose**: To display advertisements within the Game
- **Data Collected**: Advertising ID, ad interaction data, and device information
- **Privacy Policy**: [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)

### 2. RevenueCat
- **Purpose**: To process in-app purchases and manage subscriptions
- **Data Collected**: Purchase receipts, transaction information, and account identifiers
- **Privacy Policy**: [RevenueCat Privacy Policy](https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy)

### 3. Google Fonts
- **Purpose**: To provide custom fonts for the Game interface
- **Data Collected**: None (fonts are cached locally)

### 4. Google Mobile Ads SDK
- **Purpose**: To serve and manage advertisements
- **Privacy Policy**: [Google Ads Privacy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)

## Data Storage and Security

- **Local Storage**: Most game data is stored locally on your device and is not transmitted to our servers
- **No Account Required**: The Game does not require you to create an account or provide personal information
- **Data Retention**: Local game data remains on your device until you uninstall the Game or clear app data
- **Security**: We implement reasonable security measures to protect your information

## Children's Privacy

The Game is suitable for users of all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we become aware that we have inadvertently collected such information, we will take steps to delete it.

## Your Rights

You have the right to:
- **Access**: View the data stored locally on your device through your device's settings
- **Delete**: Clear all game data by uninstalling the Game or clearing app data
- **Opt-out**: Disable personalized advertising through your device settings
- **Disable AI Features**: Choose not to use the AI board generation feature

## Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any material changes by updating the "Last Updated" date and posting the new policy in the Game.
