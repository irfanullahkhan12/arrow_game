🎯 **Arrow Escape** - A neon arrow puzzle that will challenge your mind!

Every arrow has to leave the board, and it can only leave if nothing is standing in its exit ray. Tap one with a clear path and it flies off; tap a blocked one and it thuds into whatever is in the way and costs a life.

- **Ghost Arrow**: Zigzags across the board three times before escaping
- **Bomb Arrow**: Detonates everything within two cells for massive clearances
